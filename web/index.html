About the NZ version

Useful, open, and honest about being a draft.

The NZ adaptation is a draft built from published, openly licensed sources. It is not endorsed by the Ministry of Education and has not yet been reviewed by NZ teachers.

Where it comes from

Published curriculum documents

The original Marble graph includes international curriculum references; many original topics have no standard link. The 16 NZ contexts link to public sources and carry a separate editorial review status. Source records were checked on 18 October 2026.

What we have changed

16 NZ activities, with suggested year bands

NZ coins, local ecology, public transport and other home activities sit alongside the original graph. Only these additions have suggested NZ year bands. This first release supports English-medium learning; it does not cover Te Marautanga o Aotearoa or the full NZ Curriculum.

Draft mappingSource-based
What it is not

Not official, not an assessment

It is not a Ministry resource, a school report, or a test. Children learn at different paces, and a step being “later” is never a problem.

Not Ministry endorsedNot teacher reviewed
Who checks it

Open to correction

Anyone can flag a step that looks wrong. We want NZ teachers and kaiako to review the mapping, and every change is public.

Privacy & use

Keep learning personal.

What we store

Your parent username, salted password hash, hashed sessions and access credentials. We do not collect child profiles or save MCP learning inputs. Topic checkmarks stay in your browser until you clear them. The hosting provider processes network requests and limited technical logs to run the service.

Deleting your account removes its login, sessions and tokens. Do not send child names, schools, birth dates, health information or private school records through the tools. Your chosen AI provider handles conversations under its own privacy terms.

Use alongside school learning

This is a free, independent open beta for adults supporting children. Activities and NZ mappings are editorial drafts, not teacher reviewed. AI answers can be wrong. Review answers together; ask your child's teacher when a learning need is unclear.

Mathematics and English sources reflect the 2026 curriculum. Science and Social Sciences source records distinguish published 2027 content from current implementation. The original graph is primarily based on overseas curricula.

Questions or corrections: project issues. Keep personal information out of public issues.
