About the NZ version

A learning map your AI can read.

1,606 topics and 3,253 connections help your AI find the small steps behind a bigger learning goal.

How it works

Connected learning steps

The AI can search topics, check prerequisites and build a practice sequence. Each topic includes examples and questions to support learning at home.

NZ connections

Learning through everyday life

The original Marble graph is joined by 16 NZ activities, including coins, public transport and local ecology. These additions include suggested NZ years and links to their sources.

Open data

Free to use and build on

The graph and source code are open. Explore the data, suggest a correction or connect your own learning tool.

View the project

Privacy & use

Keep learning personal.

No account needed

The public MCP reads the learning graph. It does not create child profiles or save your learning questions. Topic checkmarks stay in your browser. The hosting provider processes requests and technical logs to run the service.

You stay in the conversation

Use your own AI account and review its suggestions with your child. Your AI provider handles conversations under its own terms. You can describe a learning need without sharing a child's name or school.
